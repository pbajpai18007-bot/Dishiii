Tumse baat karna, tumhari chhoti-chhoti baatein sunna, tumhara care karna, mera mood samajh lena… ye sab mere liye ordinary nahi hai, bohot precious hai. 🥹

"Mujhe tumhari woh smile pasand hai… kyunki jab tum genuinely smile karti ho, toh mujhe andar se ek ajeeb sa sukoon milta hai."

"Kuch connections ko naam dena zaroori nahi hota, bas unhe dil se feel karna enough hota hai."

Tum important ho. Tum special ho. Tumhari smile important hai. Aur sabse zyada… tum mere liye important ho. 🫶🏻
